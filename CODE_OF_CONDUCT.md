# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and
experience levels. Give feedback about code and ideas without attacking the
person who proposed them.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, sexual attention, threats, publishing someone else's private
information, and deliberately disruptive participation.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project events,
and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[the maintainer](mailto:shivamkumar872000@gmail.com). Include links or
screenshots where useful. Reports will be reviewed confidentially as far as
practical. The maintainer may remove content, warn participants, temporarily
restrict participation, or ban participants depending on the behaviour.

Do not use public issues to report private conduct concerns.
